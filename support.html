Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Sift works fully without one, and your taste stays on your device. An account is only needed to back up your Finds and to use Friends.

Why do I only hear 30 seconds?
Sift plays Apple's 30-second previews, starting at the strongest part of the song. To hear full songs, connect Apple Music on the You tab (this needs your own Apple Music subscription).

How do I save songs to Apple Music?
Open Finds and tap Save to Apple Music. Sift asks for permission once, then keeps a playlist of your Finds in your library.

How do I delete my account?
Go to the You tab and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, cloud backup, friend connections and decks straight away. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

How do I block or report someone?
In Friends, tap Block or Report on a friend or on a friend request. Blocked people can't send you requests or decks. Reports come to us and we review them.

Where do the songs come from?
Previews, artwork and the catalogue come from Apple Music. Artist information comes from the open MusicBrainz and ListenBrainz databases. Sift never downloads or stores music.
