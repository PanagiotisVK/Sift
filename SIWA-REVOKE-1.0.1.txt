SIGN IN WITH APPLE REVOKE - for 1.0.1. Written 25 Sep 2026.
======================================================================

WHY
Apple requires it (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): when someone who signed up with
Sign in with Apple deletes their account, the app must tell Apple to
revoke Sift's access. Until now Sift deleted the account but Apple still
listed Sift under Settings -> your name -> Sign in with Apple.

WHAT THE CODE DOES (already written, commit below)
  - Delete my account, for an Apple account only: Apple's sheet appears
    once (Face ID), "Confirm with Apple to disconnect Sift...".
  - The new server function apple-revoke swaps that one-time code for a
    token and revokes it with Apple.
  - Then the account is deleted as before.
  - If the user cancels the Apple sheet, or anything fails, the account is
    STILL deleted (deletion must never be blocked). The message then tells
    them how to remove Sift by hand in Settings.
  - Email accounts: no change at all.

  Files: supabase/functions/apple-revoke/index.ts
         index.html -> isAppleAccount(), revokeAppleSignIn(), deleteAccount()
