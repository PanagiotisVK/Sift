WHAT HAPPENED
Build 23 was rejected again on 24 Sep, 5.2.3 only (account deletion is
done). Apple's wording this time:

  "...potentially unauthorized access to third-party audio or video
   streaming, catalogs, and discovery services ... please ATTACH
   documentary evidence in the App Review Information section"

Our 11 Sep reply explained everything in a message. They want a FILE in
the Attachment box on the version page. The only thing there right now
is the account-deletion video.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE APP
  - The green Spotify button on Finds rows is gone. Same reason as
    YouTube: a search link needs no licence, but it can't be backed by a
    document either. Only Apple Music and Share are left.
  - The You tab has a small credits line (Apple Music, MusicBrainz,
    ListenBrainz). The MusicBrainz genre-tag licence requires it, and the
    evidence PDF points to it.
  - Also ships builds 24 and 25 (sign-up by 6-digit code, the Apple
    similar-artists graph). Testers have had both on TestFlight.

THE DOCUMENT:  Sift-5.2.3-Rights-Evidence.pdf  (in the Sift folder)
  Every third-party service, what it is used for and the licence for it,
  plus a screenshot of your MusicKit key in the developer account.
